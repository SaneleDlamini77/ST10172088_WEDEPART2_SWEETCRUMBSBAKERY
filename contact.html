Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Do you take custom orders?

Yes! We specialize in custom cakes for all occasions. Please contact us at least 72 hours in advance for custom orders.

Do you deliver?

We offer delivery within Johannesburg for orders over R300. Delivery fee is R25. Free pickup available at our bakery.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit/debit cards, and EFT payments. Payment is required when placing custom orders.
