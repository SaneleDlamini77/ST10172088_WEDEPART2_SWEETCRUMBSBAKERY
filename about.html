About Sweet Crumbs

Get to know our bakery, what we stand for, and how to reach us.

Our Story

Sweet Crumbs Bakery was founded in 2018 in Johannesburg, South Africa, as a small home-based kitchen venture. What began as a passion for baking and selling custom cakes grew into a bakery focused on fresh, memorable treats for every occasion.

Our Mission

To create delicious, high-quality baked goods using carefully selected ingredients while providing friendly service and memorable experiences for our customers.

Our Vision

To be a go-to bakery in Johannesburg for quality, taste, and memorable custom creations.
